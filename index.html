Shifts to Watch · Windows Opening Now
Booming
Social AR earns revenue
Snap paid $1.8M to Lens+ creators in October (disclosed Nov 18). Snapchat+ and Lens+ crossed 30M paid users by Nov 4, with the Lens+ tier up 600% YoY (Android Headlines). YouTube Effect Maker opened globally for monetised AR.
ARlab window: Social AR Effects and Social Gamification get priced as a paid distribution channel, not a stunt — Snap, TikTok and YouTube lenses ship with revenue-share built in for Riyadh Season, UAE National Month and FIFM.
Shift
Agents become paying customers
Anthropic listed day one at $1.76 trillion on Nov 24. ChatGPT Wallet shipped Nov 13 with Visa, Mastercard and Amex — agents can swipe the card. Canva opened its design engine to every AI agent Nov 24; Adobe shipped MCP for Photoshop and Premiere Nov 10.
ARlab window: AI Agents, Custom AI Tools, Social Media Management, Concepting and AI Consultative Solutions all ship as agent-driven defaults — strategy and creative briefs now bill in agent-hours, not slides.
Shift
AI film stack goes open
Google shipped Gemini 4 Neon Nov 24 at mid-tier pricing. Thinking Machines released an Apache-2.0 looped-MoE small enough to run on a laptop. Veo 3.1 leads 2026 video on photoreal physics. MBZUAI PAN 2 (29B, Apache-2.0) and Jais 3 (19 Arabic dialects) shipped Nov 10-13.
ARlab window: AI Creative Content, AI Brand Films, CGI and TVC rebuild on multi-model open stacks — bilingual Arabic-French-English production becomes the Q4-Q1 differentiator no single-vendor shop can match.
Open
Glasses pull ahead of headsets
IDC projects 13.6M smart-glasses units in 2026 (+167% YoY), after Counterpoint's +263% H1. Samsung opened Android XR glasses pre-order Nov 12 at $395. Meta Reality Labs Q3 revenue slid -6% to $443M; Pico Space Pro leaked for Dec 2 at $2,899.
ARlab window: Glasses briefs replace Headsets as the default wearable canvas — MR + LBE repositioned as high-value flagship stages; retail AR try-on now defaults to an always-on smart-glasses lens, not a tethered headset.
